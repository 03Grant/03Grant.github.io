About

I'm passionate about interesting things! Currently I work on the reliable behavior of large language models, and I also spend a lot of time improving the performance of AI infrastructure, especially storage.

I am a second-year PhD student in Computer Science at Purdue University, where I started in August 2025. Before Purdue, I received my B.Eng. in Software Engineering from Tongji University (2021–2025). In summer 2026 I was a research intern at Analogy AI.

Research

  • Reliable LLM behavior. Keeping a model faithful to the evidence when the user pushes back, and willing to update when the context changes.
  • Storage for AI infrastructure. Disk-based vector search that serves datasets larger than memory from commodity storage.
  • Agentic data pipelines. Agents that turn a natural-language data request into a curated training set.
