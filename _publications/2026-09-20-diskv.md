**Authors.** Yunan Zhang, Yinghao He, Jianguo Wang.

A disk-based vector search system for datasets that exceed memory capacity. It combines cluster-centroid indexing with compressed in-memory vector representations and several disk-reading methods, and uses estimated-distance filtering to exclude over 90% of candidate vectors before any disk read while maintaining retrieval accuracy.

Under review at SIGMOD 2027.
