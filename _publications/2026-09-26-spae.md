**Authors.** Yinghao He, Mengyu Xu, Haixiang Sun, Donghan Li, Yibo Wang, Lixu Wang, Kezhen Chen, Chi Li, Chunwei Liu, Bharat Bhargava, Chongyang Gao.

CoPE-Bench jointly evaluates sycophancy and contextual stubbornness across six conditions that combine user pressure with contextual evidence. SPAE is a training-free attention-steering method that uses model-identified token spans to suppress user pressure and amplify contextual evidence. Evaluated on five backbones (4B to 27B) against six baselines, SPAE reduces pressure following and improves contextual updating with no accuracy loss on no-conflict controls; on Qwen3.5-9B, pressure following drops from 24.5% to 6.0% and contextual updating rises from 50.5% to 60.2%.
